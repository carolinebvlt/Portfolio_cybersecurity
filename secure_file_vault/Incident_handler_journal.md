# Incident handler's journal

## Model 
- date
- description
- tools used
- 5 W's (Who, What, When, Where, Why)
- additional notes

### Scenario 1
#### Date 
Tuesday, July 2, 2026 at 9 AM
#### Description
A group of hackers used an email phishing campaign, which enabled the download of ransomware onto the company's network.
#### Tools used
The problem was detected when the message appeared on a screen. The existing tools therefore failed.
#### The 5 W's
- Who : A group of hackers
- What : They managed to trick an employee into downloading ransomware through a phishing campaign, which enabled the encryption of the company's data.
- When : Tuesday, July 2, 2026 at 9 AM
- Where : On an employee's computer within the company network
- Why : The employee was tricked by a phishing attempt (social engineering).
